* Searching
** Binary Search
*** Time Complexity: $$\frac{N}{2^k} \to Olog(N)$$
*** Algorithm
- find middle element
- if (target == mid) return it
- if (target > mid) search the right half
  - else search the left half
- repeat until target == mid
*** Tricks
- in *digit* arrays after the searching loop finishes:
  - the *start* will be the (*ceil*) of the target.
  - the *end* will be the (*floor*) of the target.
- *first* occurance of target will always be the *left* side (end = mid - 1).
- *last* occuracne will be in the *right* side (start = mid + 1),

* Math tricks & formulas
- Combination formula for pairs: =count * (count - 1) / 2=
